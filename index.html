About me

From commissioning machines to building for the web.

The same instincts apply in both worlds: understand the system, find the fault, and build something that works reliably.

I am a Web Application Development student with more than 10 years of experience in industrial automation, robotics and complex technical environments.

I am currently developing skills in frontend and backend development, databases, APIs, Docker and AWS cloud fundamentals. My background is built on troubleshooting under pressure, analysing complex systems, working with international teams and delivering reliable technical solutions.

I am highly motivated to transition into software development through an internship where I can continue learning, contribute to real-world projects and grow as a developer.

  • Field-tested troubleshootingDiagnosing electrical, mechanical and software issues during live start-ups and deployments.
  • International collaborationDirect work with customers and technical teams across Europe and the Americas.
  • Cloud foundationsAWS Academy Cloud Foundations in progress, with hands-on work in the AWS Learner Lab.
